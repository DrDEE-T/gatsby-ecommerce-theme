# Biscuit & Friends
## Episode 1: "Where's the Red Ball?"

Runtime: about 4.5 minutes
Audience: ages 1 to 5
Style: bright 2D cartoon, sunny back garden, big round shapes
Learning: colors, counting to 3 and 5, asking friends for help

The hook: Biscuit loses his red ball. The ball is stuck on Prickles the hedgehog's spiky back the whole time. Kids can see it. The characters can't. Every toddler watching will be shouting "It's on his back!" by the middle of the episode, and that's the point.

---

## The Cast

| Character | Who they are | Voice (free Edge voice) | Catchphrase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biscuit | Golden puppy, floppy ears, red collar. Bouncy, excited, a little dramatic when sad. | en-US-AnaNeural, normal speed. Young, bright, cute. | "Woof-woof-hooray!" |
| Mittens | Grey cat with white paws. Calm, clever, kind. The one with the plan. | en-GB-SoniaNeural, a touch slower. Warm and a bit fancy. | "Purr-fect." |
| Prickles | Round brown hedgehog with big glasses. Goofy and giggly. Everything sticks to his spikes. | en-US-RogerNeural, slightly higher pitch. Lively and silly. | "Oopsie-prickle!" |
| Narrator | Off screen. Talks to the kids. | en-US-EmmaNeural. Cheerful and clear. | "Can you help?" |

Comedy rule: one joke, repeated and building. Things keep sticking to Prickles. A flower, then a sock, then a leaf. The red ball has been there since his first scene.

---

## Sound & Music

- Biscuit: tail-wag "fwip-fwip," happy barks, paw pitter-patter
- Mittens: soft "mrrp," a purr under calm lines, a light jump "boing"
- Prickles: rolling "brrrrr" like a little wheel, a "pok!" every time something sticks, giggles
- Interactive moments: a bright xylophone ding for every correct answer and every count
- Music: bouncy ukulele theme, tiptoe plucks for searching, a hush for the bird nest, big happy finish

No music under the "tell them!" moments. Silence gives kids room to shout.

## Script

### TITLE CARD (0:00 to 0:04)

Red ball bounces in from the left, three bounces (boing, boing, boing), lands in the middle. Title pops up around it.

On screen: "BISCUIT & FRIENDS" and "Where's the Red Ball?"

Music: theme sting.

---

### SCENE 1: BALL DAY (0:04 to 0:40)

Sunny back garden. Green grass, a wooden fence, a big tree, a blue bucket, a toy box.

Biscuit runs in, tail wagging so fast his whole body wiggles.

BISCUIT: Hi, friends! I'm Biscuit! Today is the best day ever. It's ball day!

He bounces to the toy box and sticks his head inside. Rummaging sounds. A rubber bone flies out. A squeaky toy flies out.

BISCUIT (head in box): Where is it? Where is it?

He pops out. Big sad eyes. Ears droop.

BISCUIT: Oh no. My red ball is gone!

Sad trombone, soft and short.

---

### SCENE 2: MITTENS (0:40 to 1:05)

Mittens is lying on top of the fence. She stretches, yawns, and hops down. (mrrp, boing)

MITTENS: Good morning, Biscuit. Why the sad face?

BISCUIT: I lost my red ball. It's my favorite ball in the whole world.

MITTENS: Don't worry. Cats are very good at finding things. We'll find it together.

BISCUIT: Woof-woof-hooray!

---

### SCENE 3: PRICKLES ROLLS IN (1:05 to 1:30)

A rolling sound gets closer. Brrrrrr.

A brown spiky ball rolls across the garden, bonks into a flowerpot (bonk!), and pops open. It's Prickles. A pink flower is now stuck on his spikes. (pok!)

Camera note: as he stands up, his back faces the camera for a moment. The red ball is stuck on his spikes. Biscuit and Mittens are in front of him, so they can't see it. Hold the shot for 2 seconds.

PRICKLES: Oopsie-prickle! Hi, everybody!

MITTENS: Prickles, you have a flower on your spikes.

PRICKLES (looking over his shoulder the wrong way): Do I? Ooh, pretty!

Narrator, quietly, to the kids:

NARRATOR: Psst. Friends. Did you see something red?

(Pause, 2 seconds. Ball glints with a little sparkle.)

NARRATOR: Shh. Let's keep watching!

---

### SCENE 4: THE SEARCH (1:30 to 2:50)

Mittens stands up tall.

MITTENS: Here's the plan. We look in three places. Ready?

ALL THREE: Ready!

On screen: three big circles, numbered 1, 2, 3.

#### Place 1: The blue bucket

Biscuit tips over the big blue bucket. Out rolls... a yellow rubber duck. (squeak!)

BISCUIT: A duck! Not my ball.

NARRATOR: Is the red ball in the bucket?

(Pause. Kids: "No!")

NARRATOR: No! Let's keep looking.

Prickles backs up to look and bumps the duck. (pok!) Now the duck is stuck on his spikes too.

PRICKLES: Oopsie-prickle!

Circle 1 gets a check mark. (ding)

#### Place 2: The tall grass

They tiptoe into tall green grass. Tiptoe music.

MITTENS (whispering): Look carefully.

Biscuit sniffs. Sniff, sniff, sniff. He pulls out... a stripy sock.

BISCUIT: A sock! Not my ball.

NARRATOR: Is the red ball in the grass?

(Pause. Kids: "No!")

Prickles sits down to rest. Right on the sock. (pok!) Stuck.

PRICKLES: Oopsie-prickle! Again!

Circle 2 gets a check mark. (ding)

The camera passes behind Prickles again. The red ball is still there, now next to a flower, a duck and a sock. Big sparkle.

#### Place 3: Up the tree

MITTENS: Cats are good at climbing. I'll look up high.

Mittens climbs the tree (scratch, scratch) and peeks into a nest. Music goes hush.

MITTENS (whispering): Shh. It's a nest with baby birds. Let's count them, quietly.

Close-up on the nest. Three tiny sleeping birds.

NARRATOR (whispering): One. (ding, soft) Two. (ding) Three. (ding)

MITTENS (whispering): Three baby birds. But no red ball.

She climbs down very gently. Circle 3 gets a check mark.

---

### SCENE 5: BISCUIT IS SAD (2:50 to 3:20)

Biscuit sits down. Ears flop. A tiny whimper.

BISCUIT: We looked everywhere. My red ball is gone forever.

PRICKLES: Don't be sad, Biscuit! I know! I'll do my silly dance. It always makes everybody laugh!

NARRATOR: Wait! Friends, YOU know where the red ball is! Let's tell them! Say: it's on Prickles' back!

On screen, big bouncy words: "IT'S ON PRICKLES' BACK!"

(Pause, 3 seconds. No music.)

MITTENS (slowly turning to look): Prickles... turn around.

Prickles spins in a circle trying to see his own back. Faster and faster. (brrrr)

PRICKLES: I can't see my back! It's always behind me!

---

### SCENE 6: THE SILLY DANCE (3:20 to 3:50)

PRICKLES: Okay. Silly dance time!

Prickles wiggles and shakes. Music kicks in. Everything flies off his spikes one at a time, each with a pop:

- the flower (pop!)
- the duck (squeak!)
- the sock (pop!)
- and then... the RED BALL (boing!)

The ball bounces high, once, twice, three times, and lands right in front of Biscuit.

Biscuit gasps. Ears shoot up.

BISCUIT: My red ball! It was on Prickles the whole time!

PRICKLES: Oopsie-prickle! Sorry, Biscuit!

BISCUIT (laughing): That's okay! Woof-woof-hooray!

MITTENS: Purr-fect.

BISCUIT (to camera): Thank you, friends! You found it! You're super finders!

(ding, sparkle)

---

### SCENE 7: BALL GAME (3:50 to 4:15)

BISCUIT: Let's play! Count the bounces with me!

Biscuit bounces the ball. Big numbers pop up in different colors.

ALL: One! Two! Three! Four! Five!

(ding on every number)

Biscuit throws the ball to Prickles. Prickles tries to catch it... and it sticks to his spikes. (pok!)

PRICKLES: Oopsie-prickle!

Everybody laughs.

---

### SCENE 8: GOODBYE (4:15 to 4:30)

MITTENS: When you lose something, ask your friends to help.

BISCUIT: And always look behind you!

PRICKLES: Especially if you're a hedgehog!

They wave.

BISCUIT: Bye, friends! See you next time!

MITTENS: Bye-bye.

PRICKLES: Bye! Oopsie-prickle... I'm stuck to a leaf!

He rolls away with a leaf on his back. (brrrr)

End card: "BISCUIT & FRIENDS" with all three waving.

---

## Read-Aloud Check

Every line says one clear thing a 2-year-old can follow:
- The problem is said out loud: "My red ball is gone."
- The plan is said out loud: "We look in three places."
- Every search ends with a yes or no kids can shout.
- The joke is shown, not explained: things stick to Prickles.
- Lines are never spoken by two characters at the same time, except the short "Ready!" and the count, which are synced.
